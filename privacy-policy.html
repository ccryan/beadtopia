Privacy Policy for Beadtopia Marketing App

Effective Date: August 12, 2026

Beadtopia Marketing App is an internal tool used by Beadtopia to create, schedule, and review Pinterest Pins for Beadtopia’s Etsy handmade jewelry and accessories shop.

The app may access Pinterest account information needed to create and manage Pins, including board information, Pin images, Pin titles, Pin descriptions, destination links, and basic performance or reporting data.

This information is used only to create, publish, schedule, and review Pinterest content authorized by the account owner.

We do not sell personal information. We do not share user data with third parties except as necessary to operate the tool, such as sending authorized Pin content to Pinterest through the Pinterest API.

Data may be stored locally or in internal working files for the purpose of creating and managing Pins. Users may revoke Pinterest access at any time through their Pinterest account settings.

For questions or data deletion requests, contact: magikarp.feng@gmail.com
